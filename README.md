# Apple Social Media Analytics

## 📊 Project Overview

**Apple Social Media Analytics** is a data analysis project focused on evaluating Apple’s social media performance across multiple platforms, content types, hashtags, and marketing campaigns.

The project uses **Microsoft Excel** to clean, transform, analyze, and visualize social media data. Pivot tables, charts, dashboards, calculated metrics, and trend analysis were used to generate actionable insights for improving social media performance and engagement.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze social media engagement across different platforms.
* Identify the highest-performing social media platforms.
* Compare follower growth rates across platforms.
* Analyze the performance of different content types.
* Identify frequently used and high-performing hashtags.
* Evaluate campaign performance using engagement and reach metrics.
* Compare social media performance before and during campaigns.
* Analyze weekly follower growth trends.
* Use moving averages to identify underlying growth trends.
* Examine the relationship between advertising spend and follower growth.
* Present findings through interactive Excel dashboards.

## Task 1 – Data Cleaning & Preparation

The first stage focused on preparing the raw social media data for analysis.


### Data Sources

The dataset contains information such as:

* Post ID
* Platform
* Date
* Content Type
* Hashtags
* Likes
* Shares
* Comments
* Clicks
* Reach
* Impressions
* Campaign
* New Followers
* Unfollows
* Total Followers
* Engagement Rate
* Advertising Spend

### Data Cleaning Performed

* Removed duplicate records.
* Standardized date formats.
* Standardized platform names.
* Separated hashtag information.
* Formatted numerical fields.
* Formatted advertising spend as currency.
* Calculated **Net Growth**.

### Key Formula

```text
Net Growth = New Followers - Unfollows
```

```text
Growth Rate = Net Growth / Total Followers
```

# 📊 Key Insights

Based on the analysis:

### 1. Platform Performance

Twitter demonstrated strong engagement and follower growth, while YouTube showed strong engagement performance relative to advertising spend.

### 2. Content Strategy

Different platforms respond differently to content formats. Video content performed strongly across multiple platforms, while image content was effective for Twitter.

### 3. Hashtag Performance

Hashtag performance varied significantly. Analyzing clicks helped identify hashtags that could potentially contribute to stronger post performance.

### 4. Campaign Impact

Campaign periods showed higher engagement compared with pre-campaign periods for several campaigns, indicating that targeted campaigns can improve social media engagement.

### 5. Follower Growth

Weekly follower growth varied over time. Moving averages helped identify the underlying growth trend while reducing the effect of weekly fluctuations.

### 6. Multi-Platform Strategy

The analysis suggests that Apple can benefit from maintaining a **multi-platform social media strategy**, with content tailored to the strengths of each platform.

# 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. **Use a multi-platform strategy** rather than focusing on a single social media channel.
2. Create more **video content** for platforms where video demonstrates strong engagement.
3. Use **platform-specific content strategies** based on audience behavior.
4. Prioritize high-performing hashtags in relevant campaigns.
5. Monitor engagement rate and follower growth regularly.
6. Evaluate advertising spend against follower growth and engagement before increasing campaign budgets.
7. Use campaign performance data to optimize future marketing campaigns.
8. Track moving-average trends to distinguish sustainable growth from short-term fluctuations.
